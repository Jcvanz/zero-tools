Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. BannerPress works entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device — zero privacy risk.

Why WebP for Shopify banners?

WebP delivers 25–35% smaller file sizes compared to JPEG at equivalent quality. Faster images mean better Core Web Vitals scores, which directly improve your Google SEO ranking and conversion rate.

What's the ideal banner size for Shopify?

Shopify recommends 2048px wide for desktop hero banners and 1024px wide for mobile. BannerPress handles both automatically with our resize options.

What quality setting should I use?

We default to 82% — the sweet spot for e-commerce banners. It maintains sharp text and colors while reducing file size by 60–80% compared to the original PNG or JPEG.

Does Shopify support WebP images?

Yes! Shopify has natively supported WebP since 2020. All modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) also fully support WebP.
