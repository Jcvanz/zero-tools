Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2025

BannerPress ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website bannerpress.app. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

1. No Image Data Collection

BannerPress processes all images entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images are never uploaded to our servers. We do not store, transmit, or have access to any images you process with this tool.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following non-personal information automatically via analytics services:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring URL
  • Approximate geographic region (country/city level)

This data is collected in aggregate form and cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymized traffic data. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies only through third-party services (Google Analytics, Google AdSense). You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@bannerpress.app
